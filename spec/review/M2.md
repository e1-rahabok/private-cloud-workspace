## Purpose and reason

The adapter that reads VCF Operations, Agent E2's knowledge, PCM and isvara.io each hold some of the same facts: a KPI's name, its limits, its score bands and the level at which a cluster closes for business. When two copies differ, a customer sees one KPI under two names or with two scores. M2 makes Agent E2's knowledge the master copy and adds an automatic check, run on every change in every repository, that fails when a copy differs. It also tidies the five repositories so that a newcomer finds things where their names say: one name for the product, folders named for what they hold, old benchmarks and repeated knowledge pages gone, no lab address or password in any file, and old branches deleted without anyone's help.

For customers and visitors, nothing yet: M2 adds no feature. For you, a KPI carries the same name, limits and score bands in the adapter, in Agent E2 and in PCM, and an automatic check fails when they differ. The rebuilt isvara.io runs beside the live one at its own address for you to compare; search engines do not list it, and the live site is untouched. You no longer delete old branches.
